HOW DID THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE EMERGE?

English comes from the Germanic language family. It developed from the languages spoken by the Angles, Saxons, and Jutes who arrived in Britain in the 5th century. Over time, this language became the main language of the people living there and continued to grow. In 1066, when the Normans conquered England, many French words were added to English. Later, English also borrowed words from Latin and other European languages. Through these historical changes, English reached its modern form and became a widely spoken language around the world.

WHY IS THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE SO IMPORTANT?

English is an important skill that helps individuals communicate with the world more easily. This language increases a person’s access to information, develops their way of thinking, and helps them understand different cultures better. While it supports success in education, it also offers wider opportunities in working life. At the same time, it increases self-confidence and strengthens the ability to express oneself. In general, learning English contributes to a person’s personal development and makes it easier to achieve future goals.

WHERE IS ENGLISH USED?

English is one of the most widely used languages in the world today and is an important means of communication in many different fields. In education, it is often preferred in universities, scientific research, and academic publications; in this way, people from different countries can share information in a common language. In the business world, it is used as the language of communication especially in international companies, foreign trade, and meetings. In the tourism sector, it makes it easier for people to understand each other in hotels, airports, and guiding services. In the field of technology, a large number of computer programs, mobile applications, and websites are prepared in English. In addition, it is widely used in cultural and entertainment areas such as cinema, music, TV series, and games. In short, English is a common language that enables people to communicate with each other in many areas, from daily life to the world of science.

HOW DO YOU FORM A SENTENCE?

The basic structure is Subject + Verb + Object. If people do not know this order, forming sentences becomes very difficult for them.

  • 1 Choose the Subject: Determine the person or thing that performs the action in the sentence.
  • 2 Use the verb in the correct form: Choose a verb that matches the tense and the subject.
  • 3 Determine the tense: Decide whether the tense is past, present, or future.
  • 4 Add the Object: Write the thing that is affected by the action (if there is one).
  • 5 Add place and time expressions (optional): Answer the questions “Where?” and “When?”.
  • 6 Check the sentence: Review the word order (S + V + O) and the grammar.
